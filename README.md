# Baymax — AI Healthcare Companion

An AI health companion inspired by Baymax (Big Hero 6): describe how you're feeling, and a small team of AI agents work together to give you grounded, cited guidance from real public-health sources (CDC / WHO / MedlinePlus), an urgency level, and — when needed — real nearby care options.

> **Disclaimer:** Fan project inspired by Big Hero 6 — not affiliated with or endorsed by Disney. This is a portfolio/educational project, not a medical device — it does not diagnose conditions and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call your local emergency number.

🚧 In progress — foundation and RAG pipeline being built.

- **Frontend:** React + TypeScript + Tailwind + shadcn/ui (coming later)
