TITLE: Sore Throat
SOURCE: CDC (https://www.cdc.gov/sore-throat/about/index.html)
RETRIEVED: 2026-09-15

## Causes
Most sore throats are from viral infections (colds, flu). Other causes: group A Streptococcus bacteria (strep throat), allergies, smoking/secondhand smoke.

## Symptoms
Painful swallowing, dry/scratchy throat. Viral sore throats may also include cough, runny nose, hoarseness, or pink eye — less typical of strep throat.

## Self-Care Strategies
Ice chips or popsicles, humidifier or cool mist device, salt water gargle, warm fluids and hydration, honey for cough relief (ages 1+), OTC pain relievers as directed. Most sore throats resolve within a week without antibiotics — "Antibiotics will not help" if caused by a virus.

## When to Seek Medical Care
Difficulty breathing or swallowing, blood in saliva or phlegm, excessive drooling (young children), dehydration, joint swelling and pain, rash, symptoms persisting beyond a few days or worsening, recurrent sore throats.

## Immediate Attention Required
Infants under 3 months with a fever of 100.4°F or higher.
