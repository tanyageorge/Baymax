## What Is a Sprain?
"A sprain is a stretched or torn ligament." Ligaments connect bones at joints. Common causes: falling, twisting, direct impact. Ankle and wrist sprains are frequent. Symptoms: pain, swelling, bruising, reduced joint mobility, sometimes a popping/tearing sensation at the moment of injury.

## What Is a Strain?
"A strain is a stretched or torn muscle or tendon." Tendons connect muscles to bones. Can develop suddenly or gradually through twisting/pulling. Back and hamstring strains are common, especially in athletes. Symptoms: pain, muscle spasms, swelling, difficulty moving the affected muscle.

## Initial Treatment (RICE approach)
"Treatment of both sprains and strains usually involves resting the injured area, icing it, wearing a bandage or device that compresses the area, and medicines." Later stages may add exercise and physical therapy to restore function and prevent future injury.

## When to Seek Medical Care
This information "should not be used as a substitute for professional medical care or advice." Seek medical evaluation for significant inability to move the joint, extreme swelling, or persistent pain despite home treatment.
