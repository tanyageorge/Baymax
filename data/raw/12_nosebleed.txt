TITLE: Nosebleed
SOURCE: MedlinePlus (https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003106.htm)
RETRIEVED: 2026-09-15

## Home First Aid
Sit down and gently squeeze the soft part of the nose between thumb and finger. Lean forward to avoid swallowing blood and breathe through the mouth. Wait at least 10 minutes before checking if bleeding has stopped. Cold compresses across the bridge of the nose may help. Do not pack gauze inside the nose. Avoid lying down during a nosebleed. For several hours after bleeding stops, avoid sniffing or blowing the nose. A nasal decongestant spray may help control persistent bleeding.

## Prevention Tips
Keep indoor air cool and moist (vaporizer); use nasal saline spray and water-soluble jelly to prevent drying, especially in winter.

## Seek Emergency Care Immediately If:
Bleeding continues beyond 20 minutes; the nosebleed follows a head injury (possible skull fracture); the nose appears broken or crooked after injury; the person takes blood-thinning medication; there's a history of nosebleeds requiring specialist treatment.

## See a Doctor If:
Frequent nosebleeds occur; the nose may be broken; nosebleeds occur without a cold or minor irritation as a cause; bleeding develops after sinus or other surgery.
