## What Is Anaphylaxis?
A serious allergic reaction that develops rapidly and can be life-threatening — a severe immune response to a triggering substance.

## Common Causes
Food allergies (particularly peanuts), medications, stinging insect reactions, latex exposure, physical exercise, and sometimes unknown causes.

## Symptoms by Body System
Skin: itching, hives, redness, swelling.
Respiratory: shortness of breath, coughing, wheezing, throat tightness with difficulty swallowing.
Nasal/oral: sneezing, stuffy/runny nose, lip or tongue swelling.
Cardiovascular: weak pulse, fainting, shock.
Gastrointestinal: vomiting, diarrhea, cramping.
Neurological: dizziness or fainting.
Chest: pain or tightness.

## When to Seek Emergency Care
"If someone is having a serious allergic reaction, call 911." Administer an auto-injector (epinephrine) immediately if available. Any combination of the symptoms above warrants emergency medical attention, since anaphylaxis can progress rapidly and become life-threatening without prompt treatment.
