TITLE: Insect Bites and Stings
SOURCE: MedlinePlus (https://www.medlineplus.gov/insectbitesandstings.html)
RETRIEVED: 2026-09-15

## Home Care and Prevention
Avoid contact with insects; use insect repellent; wear protective clothing outdoors; take precautions when eating outside since food attracts insects.

## When to See a Doctor
Consider consulting a doctor/dermatologist if a bite or sting shows signs of complications or fails to improve with home care.

## Emergency Warning Signs
People with known severe allergic reactions to insect bites/stings (anaphylaxis risk) should carry an emergency epinephrine kit. Seek emergency care immediately if signs of anaphylaxis or another severe allergic reaction develop after a bite or sting.

## Disease Risk
Mosquito bites can transmit West Nile virus in the US; in other regions, mosquitoes and other insects may carry malaria and other infections — a consideration for travelers.
