## What Causes Headaches
Tension headaches are the most common type, caused by "tight muscles in your shoulders, neck, scalp and jaw." Risk factors include overwork, insufficient sleep, skipped meals, and alcohol consumption; often linked to stress, depression, or anxiety. Other common types include migraines, cluster headaches, and sinus headaches.

## Self-Care and Home Treatment
Most people find relief through lifestyle changes, relaxation techniques, and pain relievers. Guidance includes managing tension-type headaches with self-care at home, dietary adjustments (e.g. low-tyramine diet for some people), and "headache hygiene" strategies.

## When to Seek Medical Attention
Contact your doctor if you experience sudden, severe headaches.

## Seek Emergency Care Immediately If:
Headache after a blow to the head, or headache along with a stiff neck, fever, confusion, loss of consciousness, or pain in the eye or ear. These may indicate a serious underlying condition requiring urgent evaluation.
